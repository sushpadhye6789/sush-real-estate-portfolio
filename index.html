  • Branded search drove the most cost-effective lead of any paid channel — a blended $30.04 CPL across all four states.
  • Suburb-specific ad group restructuring cut CPC by 20% in Q3 2020.
  • Home & Land campaigns consistently drove the highest-volume, lowest-CPC traffic — the basis for expanding them further.
  • Early SEO fixes already moved the needle: bounce rate down 11.2%, pages per session up 16.8%, load time down 12.6%.

Why SEO mattered here

Organic was already the 3rd-highest traffic channel at 21% of the mix, and Google's page-1 results captured 92% of all organic search traffic — the strategy laid technical foundations first, then content and backlinks, to convert that ceiling into share.

Competitive gap vs. Metricon

Metricon held ~1,600 keywords in Top 3 positions to Burbank's 112, and grew organic traffic from 118K to 193.7K between Sep 2019 and Feb 2021. Burbank's own organic traffic still grew 15.9K → 20.3K on fewer ranking keywords — proof positions, not just volume, had improved.

The plan treated SEM and SEO as one system, not two budgets: SEO for CPA, SEM for volume — paid search buys the clicks organic hasn't earned yet, while every technical and content fix compounds the cost of those clicks down.

Strategy Refresh — Burbank Homes

2021 · National

Six years into the Burbank relationship, a strategic reset — the housing market was moving faster than at any point in a decade, and the plan needed to rebuild media, creative and audience targeting around three buyer types instead of one generic "home buyer".

First home buyers

73% call home-buying research a massive hurdle; 59% plan to enter with under a 20% deposit. The average FHB age is rising, expanding the addressable audience from 330K to 480K potential reach at 20–45.

Second home buyers

Downsizing activity is up 2.5x YoY, and interstate relocation for cheaper housing is accelerating — ~9,000 NSW and ~14,200 VIC residents planned a move to QLD, SA, TAS or WA within 12 months.

  • One in five Australians owns an investment property, and investors are moving decisively online for research — an opening for content-led engagement on cash flow, gearing and yields.
  • Burbank's spend per dwelling sits well below Metricon, Carlisle and Henley, making channel efficiency — not raw budget — the lever for share of voice.
  • Three brand pillars anchor every message: we care and connect with our customers, we're experts at building Aussie homes, we innovate.
  • Three creative platforms were developed to bring the pillars to life: You're Invited (experience the home before you commit), The Promise (hero the tradespeople behind every build), and Matchmaker (match the house to how a family actually lives).

The refresh's real shift: stop marketing one generic home buyer and start running three distinct journeys — first home buyers, second home buyers and investors — each with its own insight, channel mix and creative idea.
